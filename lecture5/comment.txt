I watched this movie last weekend with a group of friends. At the beginning, everything felt amazing and exciting. The opening scenes were well-designed, and the music was good. However, after about thirty minutes, the story became slow and confusing. Some parts were clearly boring, and a few scenes were even worse than expected. One of my friends said the acting was not bad, but I think it was actually quite bad in several moments. The dialogue felt unnatural, and the plot development was terrible at times. There were also some boring conversations that did not contribute anything to the story. On the positive side, the cinematography was beautiful, and a few moments were truly touching. But overall, the experience was disappointing. I would not say the movie is awful, but it is definitely not good either. In fact, some scenes were so bad that people in the theater started laughing. In conclusion, while the movie had potential, it failed to deliver a satisfying experience. It is not the worst movie ever, but it is still boring and occasionally terrible.
